Welcome home

Three things we do well.

The House Café is built on a simple idea: good food and drinks, served somewhere that feels like your own living room.

Coffee

From a classic hot Americano to iced lemonade and fragrant teas — Earl Grey, English Breakfast and Jasmine Green.

Cake

Our Basque cheesecake — caramelised outside, creamy inside — is the perfect afternoon pick-me-up with your cup.

Comfort

Pork free and alcohol free, with mock-meat favourites — so friends and family can all share the same table.

Our menu

Toasted, stacked & made fresh.

Hearty sandwiches on toast, croissants and bagels — filled with mock "chicken", scrambled egg, cheese and crisp coral lettuce.
